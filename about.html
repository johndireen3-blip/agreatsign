About A Great Sign

A study of biblical prophecy for Catholics and for anyone who wants to read the end of the story the way the Church reads it: with sober attention to the text, with the Fathers and saints, and with hope.

Why this site exists

Prophecy draws people. It also draws confusion, fear and date-setting. This site goes through the whole Bible, the apparitions the Church has examined and the writings of the Fathers and saints, so that readers can see what God has already done, what he is doing now, and what we still await, each with the weight it actually carries.

“The goal is not to provoke fear, but to offer hope.”Fr. John

Who does what

A Great Sign was founded and is directed by Fr. John, a Catholic diocesan priest, as part of The Ephesus Project, his family of websites on the Book of Revelation and Christian hope. Its companion, The Lamb's Victory, reads the Book of Revelation chapter by chapter.

Fr. John chose the subject, sets the study's purpose and rules, and directs the work. The pages are researched and written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, under his direction. Fr. John's own reflections, such as The Temple of His Body, are marked as his. The authority rests with Scripture, Tradition and the Magisterium. How this site is made explains the rules Claude follows and how sources are checked.

An independent project. Fr. John is a Catholic diocesan priest, but this project is his own and is not affiliated with or endorsed by his diocese, the USCCB, Anthropic, or any other organization. It does not replace the teaching of the Church.

How this site reads prophecy

With the Fathers

The first Christian writers are our guides. Where they agree, their reading carries weight; where they differ, both views are shown.

Soberly & Vigilant

No date-setting and no sensationalism. “Of that day and hour no one knows” (Matthew 24:36; Catechism 673). Vigilance! “Be sober and vigilant. Your opponent the devil is prowling around like a roaring lion looking for [someone] to devour” (1 Peter 5:8).

Historically

Every passage is read first in its own setting, by its own author, for its first readers, before asking how it looks forward.

Rules of the road

  • Scripture is quoted from the New American Bible, Revised Edition, with links to the full text at bible.usccb.org/bible.
  • Levels of authority are always labeled: Scripture, defined Church teaching, the Catechism and Magisterium, the Fathers and Doctors, saints, approved theologians, and Fr. John's own reflection, which is always marked as his.
  • Private revelation stays in its place. Approved apparitions help us live the Gospel; they never add to it (Catechism 67).
  • Quotations from saints and teachers come from sources you can check, and each is cited.
  • Other readings, Catholic and otherwise, are presented fairly before any conclusion is drawn.

About the logo

The mark is drawn from Revelation 12. “But the woman was given the two wings of the great eagle, so that she could fly to her place in the desert, where, far from the serpent, she was taken care of” (Revelation 12:14). The wings carry a golden sun, for the woman “clothed with the sun,” crowned with “a crown of twelve stars” (Revelation 12:1). Beneath them lies the desert, the place God prepared for her. The Church reads the woman as Israel, Mary and the Church, sheltered and fed by God through every trial. The logo was designed with Claude at Fr. John's direction.
