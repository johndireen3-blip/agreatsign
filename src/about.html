About A Great Sign

A study of biblical prophecy for Catholics and for anyone who wants to read the end of the story the way the Church reads it: with sober attention to the text, with the Fathers and saints, and with hope.

Why this site exists

Prophecy draws people. It also draws confusion, fear and date-setting. This site goes through the whole Bible, the apparitions the Church has examined and the writings of the Fathers and saints, so that readers can see what God has already done, what he is doing now, and what we still await, each with the weight it actually carries.

“The goal is not to provoke fear, but to offer hope.”Fr. John

The author

A Great Sign is written by Fr. John, a diocesan Catholic priest. He also writes The Lamb's Victory, a chapter-by-chapter Catholic study of the Book of Revelation. This site is offered for prayerful study and is not an official publication of any diocese or Church body.

How this site reads prophecy

With the Fathers

The first Christian writers are our guides. Where they agree, their reading carries weight; where they differ, both views are shown.

Soberly

No date-setting and no sensationalism. “Of that day and hour no one knows” (Matthew 24:36; Catechism 673).

Historically

Every passage is read first in its own setting, by its own author, for its first readers, before asking how it looks forward.

Rules of the road

  • Scripture is quoted from the New American Bible, Revised Edition, with links to the full text at bible.usccb.org.
  • Levels of authority are always labeled: Scripture, defined Church teaching, the Catechism and Magisterium, the Fathers and Doctors, saints, approved theologians, and the author's own reading.
  • Private revelation stays in its place. Approved apparitions help us live the Gospel; they never add to it (Catechism 67).
  • Quotations from saints and teachers come from sources you can check, and each is cited.
  • Other readings, Catholic and otherwise, are presented fairly before any conclusion is drawn.
